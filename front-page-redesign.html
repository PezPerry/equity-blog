All eight readings, the method and its limits
MarketSessionCloseChangeWeatherReadingStatus

These are completed daily closes, refreshed once a day at about 07:00 London time. They are not live prices. The change compares the latest two completed sessions. Each market's score is that change divided by twice its own volatility over up to 60 earlier sessions, limited to between −1 and +1, and the score sets the weather. The VIX score is reversed, so easing anxiety brightens the sky.

The data comes from a free feed that can be late, corrected or missing. Futures prices can jump when the front contract rolls, daily closes are not guaranteed official settlements, and holiday handling for futures and currencies is approximate. Pound strength is an equal-weight basket of sterling against the dollar, euro, yen, Swiss franc, Canadian dollar and Australian dollar; it is not an official sterling index.
