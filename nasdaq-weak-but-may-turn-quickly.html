The Nasdaq is weak, but it may turn quickly

US inflation eased less than expected in October. Prices rose 0.4% against September, down from 0.6% the month before, but the expectation was 0.3%.

They're not that different, but it points further towards the potential for greater interest rate rises in the United States. The core measure, which strips out food and energy, rose 0.3% for a third month running, against the 0.2% expected.

Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller has suggested that if core inflation doesn't cool, the central bank should consider raising rates by half a percentage point next month. He set the line at a core reading of around 0.3%, and that's where the figure landed.

The Nasdaq

That has an impact on markets, and today the Nasdaq 100 continues to be on weak ground, down -0.60% on the day to close at 27,741.

It's worth bearing in mind that the Nasdaq has fallen quite considerably since early October. From the record close of 31,225 on 6 October the index is down -11.2%, and that includes a fall of -6.4% in a single session on 5 November.

How quickly this can change

Something I would say, though, is that market sentiment on this can change quite quickly.

The inflationary issue is driven by the elevated cost of energy, and this has been as a result of the Iranian crisis.

It's extremely hard to estimate what Donald Trump will do next. I've long expected the Iranian situation to simply get resolved one way or another, because he is capable of reversing course on a whim. The fact that he hasn't already done so does surprise me.

On Monday he said the war could end “fairly soon”, and Brent crude fell below $90 a barrel this morning for the first time since August.

You could expect concerns about longer-term inflation to be somewhat reduced if the Iranian conflict comes to an abrupt end.

The AI trade

One of the core driving parts of the AI trade is that it is very much the future, and the growth figures coming out of these companies are monumental. I won't underestimate the impact of those on the market in the coming months.

So the Nasdaq is very weak right now, but it may turn quite quickly and quite dramatically.
