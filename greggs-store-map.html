Reading the map. Coverage is already close to blanket across the North East heartland, Yorkshire, the Midlands, the central belt of Scotland, London and South Wales. The 3,500-shop ambition therefore rests on the white space between the dots — petrol forecourts, supermarkets, retail parks, travel hubs, hospitals and universities, plus smaller formats where a full shop can’t justify itself; over half of new openings are already beyond the high street. Greggs doesn’t publish opening dates for individual shops, so shops opened in the last twelve months can’t be reliably shown in a different colour.
